Cold water and your health

Cold water is dangerous. It can cause cold shock, loss of breath control, hypothermia and heart rhythm problems. Floe is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

  • Water temperatures, forecasts, the best window and the time-in-water guide are estimates. Conditions at your spot can be different — check them yourself before you go in.
  • Heart rate and recovery shown by Floe come from Apple Watch and are for your information only.
  • Talk to a doctor before you start cold-water dipping, especially if you have a heart or blood-pressure condition or are pregnant.
  • Never dip alone, stay close to the shore, follow local rules and warnings, and get out as soon as you feel unwell.

You use Floe and go into cold water at your own risk.
